Who We Are

A Non-Profit Rooted in
Community & Compassion

Founded in 2016, WARN Nepal (Women Agency Research Center Nepal) is a registered non-profit organization. We support communities in times of disaster, offer scholarships for underprivileged students, and work for women empowerment across Nepal.

Why We Exist

Our Purpose

Guided by strong values, we work toward a Nepal where every woman can live with dignity, safety, and opportunity.

Vision

A Nepal where women have equal rights, equal opportunities, and the freedom to shape their own futures — free from discrimination and violence.

Mission

As a non-profit, we deliver disaster relief, scholarships for underprivileged learners, and women empowerment programs — building resilient individuals and communities across Nepal.

Values

Dignity, Inclusion, Transparency, Community, and Courage guide everything we do at WARN Nepal.
