WARN Nepal – Biodata Folder
===========================

The PDFs here are GENERATED by make-biodata.bat from the details
in tools\make-biodata.js (photos come from images\team\).

To change someone's biodata:
  1. Edit their entry in tools\make-biodata.js
  2. Double-click make-biodata.bat
  3. The PDF in this folder is rebuilt

(or drop a ready-made PDF here with the name above), then in
js\main.js → IMAGES.biodata replace null with 'biodata/<slug>.pdf'.
